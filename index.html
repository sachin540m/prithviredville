Live Charcoal Barbecue & Unlimited Feast

Food is a central highlight of any memorable countryside escape. At Prithvi’s Redville, evenings come alive around the glowing embers of our live outdoor barbecue grill—serving up sizzling, spiced chicken skewers, charred paneer tikka, and farm-fresh roasted veggies marinated to perfection.

Pair your barbecue with our grand, unlimited home-style feast featuring authentic Maharashtrian Chicken Gravy (Rassa), flavorful dry chicken, paneer specialties, hot chapatis, and steaming rice. Enjoy dining in our open garden and poolside seating under the valley stars with river breeze and bonfire.

Nearby Attractions & Sightseeing

Located right in the lap of nature, here are the most scenic spots located just minutes away from our property.

1.5 KM Away

Gadeshwar Dam
& Reservoir

A serene freshwater reservoir flanked by rolling Western Ghats ridges, calm river backwaters, and magical sunset viewpoints right in our neighborhood.

1 KM Away

Dhodani Secret Waterfalls

Natural seasonal cascades located right within Dhodani village. Perfect for a refreshing nature walk, photography, and bathing in cool mountain waters.

Starts from Village

Dhodani-Matheran
Sunset Trail

Maharashtra's famous historic hiking route starting directly from Dhodani village, ascending scenic forest paths up to Matheran's Sunset Point.

12 KM Away

Kalavantin Durg
& Prabalgad

Panvel's legendary historical pinnacle fortress known across India for thrilling rock-cut steps and breathtaking 360-degree Western Ghat views.

18 KM Away

Karnala Bird
Sanctuary & Fort

A protected lush forest reserve home to over 150 rare bird species, ancient hill fort ruins, and serene green trails just a short drive away.

Our Location & Proximity

Prithvi’s Redville is conveniently located in Dhodani Village, right in front of Bajaj Farm on Pimplwadi Road, near Gadeshwar Dam, Panvel. The property enjoys peaceful rural surroundings with direct river-touch and mesmerizing valley views while remaining easily reachable by road or railway.

Address: Prithvi’s Redville, Dhodani Village, In front of Bajaj Farm, Pimplwadi Road, Near Gadeshwar Dam, Dodhani Taluka, Panvel, Maharashtra.
Railway Proximity: 12 km from Panvel Railway Station (approx. 20-minute auto/cab drive).
From Mumbai / Navi Mumbai: Approx. 1 to 1.5 hours drive (45-60 km) via Sion-Panvel Highway.
From Pune: Approx. 2 hours drive (110 km) via Mumbai-Pune Expressway (Panvel / Shedung exit).

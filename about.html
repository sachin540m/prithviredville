Our Story & Philosophy

Where Cool Mountain Air Meets Gentle River Breeze

Tucked away in the serene countryside of Dhodani Village near the iconic Gadeshwar Dam, Prithvi’s Redville was created with a clear purpose: to provide families, friends, and teams an unpolluted nature retreat away from the noise and pressure of urban life.

Situated just 12 km from Panvel Railway Station, our property is gifted with pure mountain air drifting down from the misty peaks of Matheran, with a natural river flowing right alongside our boundary. Designed specifically for group getaways, we follow an exclusive booking model—meaning your party enjoys private access to the huge swimming pool, lawns, and entertainment amenities without sharing with strangers.

Whether you are celebrating a milestone birthday, hosting a college reunion, arranging a family get-together, or organizing a productive corporate team offsite, we combine open nature with genuine warmth, high-bass DJ entertainment, and unlimited authentic home-cooked meals.

Good To Know

House Rules & Guest Guidelines

To ensure a safe, enjoyable, and seamless experience for all visitors, we kindly ask our guests to review our standard policies.

  • Standard Timings: Check-in begins at 5:00 PM and check-out is at 3:00 PM the following day for our 22-hour package. Early arrival or late departure is subject to prior host confirmation.
  • Swimming Pool Safety: The private pool is available throughout your stay. Proper swimwear is advised. Children must be accompanied and supervised by adults at all times.
  • Music & DJ Sound System: Guests are welcome to play music and celebrate. In accordance with countryside and local environmental regulations, late-night music volumes must be adjusted to respectful ambient levels.
  • Unlimited Meal Policy: All meal packages include 100% unlimited servings during dinner and lunch. Barbecue grill facilities are available on request for evening gatherings.
  • ID Verification: A valid government-issued photo ID (Aadhar Card, Driving License, or Passport) is required for primary guests at the time of check-in.

# Prithvi's Redville Farmhouse & Nature Retreat

Official website for **Prithvi's Redville**, a private 22-hour river-touch farmhouse and nature sanctuary located near Gadeshwar Dam, Panvel (12 km from Panvel Station).

## 🌿 Overview
- **Location:** Dhodani Village, In front of Bajaj Farm, Pimplwadi Road, Near Gadeshwar Dam, Panvel, Maharashtra.
- **Stay Package:** Exclusive 22-hour full stay (Check-in: 5:00 PM | Check-out: 3:00 PM next day).
- **Amenities:** Private Swimming Pool, High-Bass DJ Sound System, Lush Party Lawns, River Access & Check Dam, AC Master Bedrooms, Spacious Group Dormitory, Evening Bonfire & Live Barbecue, Unlimited Veg & Non-Veg Meals.

## 🚀 Website Architecture
Built with pure **HTML5, CSS3, and Vanilla JavaScript** — zero frameworks or build tools needed. 100% ready for instant deployment on **Hostinger (`public_html`)**, **GitHub Pages**, **Netlify**, or **Vercel**.

### Pages
1. `index.html` - Home Page featuring full-bleed drone hero banner, floating availability bar, occasions breakdown, culinary showcase, expanding nearby attractions gallery, 22-hour itinerary timeline, pricing table, Google reviews, FAQs, and Google Map.
2. `about.html` - Story and philosophy, 4 pillars of hospitality, group privacy highlights, and property guidelines.
3. `amenities.html` - Complete breakdown of amenities, full food menu (Breakfast, Lunch, Hi-Tea, Dinner, BBQ), and flexible package pricing.
4. `gallery.html` - Curated photo & video gallery with 47 high-resolution photos and 8 video walkthroughs, instant category filter tabs, uniform luxury cards, and full-screen lightbox modal.
5. `contact.html` - Interactive WhatsApp booking enquiry form, location details, turn-by-turn driving directions from Mumbai/Pune, and interactive Google Map.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack
- **HTML5:** Semantic, accessible markup.
- **CSS3:** Custom responsive layout, CSS Grid, Flexbox, glassmorphism UI, smooth cubic-bezier transitions.
- **JavaScript:** Pure Vanilla JS for mobile hamburger menu, navbar scroll transitions, category filter tabs, interactive accordion, and fullscreen lightbox modal.
- **Fonts & Icons:** Google Fonts (Cinzel & Plus Jakarta Sans), Font Awesome 6.5.1.

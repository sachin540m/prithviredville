Locate Prithvi's Redville

Conveniently situated in Dhodani Village near Gadeshwar Dam, only 12 km from Panvel Station.

By Train (Panvel Station)

Board any Harbour or Central line local/express train to Panvel Railway Station. From the station, our farmhouse is just 12 km away. Auto-rickshaws, private cabs, and local transit are readily available outside the station.

By Car from Mumbai / Navi Mumbai

Drive via the Sion-Panvel Expressway towards Panvel. Proceed onto New Panvel / Gadeshwar Dam Road towards Dhodani Village. Smooth, scenic roadway flanked by lush greenery and hills.

By Road from Pune

Drive via the Mumbai-Pune Expressway, take the exit at Shedung toll / Panvel, and proceed towards Gadeshwar Dam road directly to Dhodani. Total driving time approximately 2 hours.

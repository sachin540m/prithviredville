Travel Guide

How To Reach Prithvi's Redville

Conveniently accessible from Mumbai, Navi Mumbai, Thane, and Pune.

By Train (Panvel Station)

Board any Harbour or Central line train to Panvel Railway Station. From Panvel station, our property is just 12 km away. Auto-rickshaws, private cabs, and local transport are readily available outside the station.

By Car from Mumbai / Navi Mumbai

Take the Sion-Panvel Expressway towards Panvel. Turn towards New Panvel / Gadeshwar Dam Road towards Dhodani Village. Smooth scenic road flanked by nature and greenery.

By Road from Pune

Drive via the Mumbai-Pune Expressway, exit at Panvel / Shedung toll, and proceed towards Gadeshwar Dam road to Dhodani. Total driving time approximately 2 hours.

Landmark: Near Gadeshwar Dam

Our farmhouse is located in Dhodani Village, right in front of Bajaj Farm on Pimplwadi Road, offering breathtaking panoramic valley views of Matheran.

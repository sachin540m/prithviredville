Guest Policies & Guidelines

Terms & Conditions

Please read our guest stay policies and property guidelines to ensure a safe, enjoyable, and seamless 22-hour experience for your group.

Commitment to Privacy & Safety

At Prithvi's Redville Farmhouse, we operate on an exclusive booking model — meaning when your group books, the entire property, swimming pool, lawns, and facilities remain 100% private to your party without any strangers. These terms are established to preserve property hygiene, guest safety, and peaceful community surroundings.

1. Check-in & Check-out Policies

  • 22-Hour Stay Package: Check-in is strictly at 5:00 PM and Check-out is at 3:00 PM the following afternoon.
  • One-Day Return Trips: Day outing packages operate between 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM.
  • Early check-in or late check-out is strictly subject to prior written approval and slot availability, and may incur additional hourly charges.

2. Booking, Advance & Cancellation

  • A booking is only deemed confirmed once the requisite advance token deposit has been received and acknowledged via official WhatsApp or call.
  • The remaining balance amount must be settled in full at the time of check-in via UPI, bank transfer, or cash.
  • Cancellations requested at least 7 days prior to check-in may be eligible for date rescheduling subject to calendar availability. Token deposits are non-refundable for last-minute cancellations or no-shows.

3. Mandatory Identification Proof

  • As mandated by local authorities and law enforcement, all adult guests must present a valid government-issued photo ID (Aadhaar Card, Driving License, or Passport) at check-in.
  • The lead guest who initiated the booking bears legal responsibility for all individuals in their group.

4. Swimming Pool Safety & Hygiene Rules

  • Proper Swimwear: Synthetic nylon or lycra swimwear is required for pool entry to maintain water hygiene and filtration efficiency.
  • No Glassware: Glass bottles, glass tumblers, and sharp objects are strictly prohibited near or inside the swimming pool deck. Please use reusable paper or plastic cups.
  • Children's Safety: Children under 12 must be accompanied and supervised by an adult at all times around the pool area. There is no lifeguard on duty; guests swim at their own risk.
  • Diving in shallow pool sections or entering the pool under extreme intoxication is strictly forbidden.

5. Music System & Sound Regulations

  • Our high-power DJ sound system is provided for your party enjoyment on the lawn and patio.
  • In compliance with local rural tranquility guidelines and Panvel police environmental regulations, outdoor music volume must be moderated after 10:00 PM. Guests may continue listening to music indoors at enjoyable ambient volumes.

6. Food & Meal Dining Timings

  • Welcome Refreshments: Served on arrival (tea & snacks) between 5:30 PM - 6:30 PM.
  • Dinner: Freshly prepared unlimited buffet served from 8:30 PM - 10:30 PM.
  • Morning Breakfast: Fresh tea/coffee with Poha / Misal Pav / Bhurji Pav served after 10:00 AM.
  • Lunch: Unlimited vegetarian and non-vegetarian feast served from 1:00 PM - 2:30 PM prior to 3:00 PM check-out.
  • All meals are prepared fresh on-site by our village cooks. Outside catering requires prior approval.

7. Property Care & Damage Liability

  • Guests are expected to treat the property, furnishings, appliances, and outdoor amenities with utmost care and respect.
  • Any physical damage caused to the premises, sound equipment, furniture, bedding, or pool mechanisms during the stay will be assessed and billed directly to the lead guest prior to checkout.
  • Littering in the natural river stream or lawn is strictly discouraged; please dispose of all trash in provided garbage bins.

8. Personal Belongings & Liability

  • Guests are solely responsible for the safekeeping of their personal valuables, cash, electronic gadgets, and vehicles parked on the premises. Management holds no liability for loss or theft.
  • Illegal substances, narcotics, and firearms are strictly prohibited on the property. Violation will result in immediate termination of the stay and reporting to Panvel police authorities.
